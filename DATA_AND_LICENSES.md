# Data, attribution and public-release boundaries

This repository is a public project overview. It contains selected, non-georeferenced
screenshots and a compressed demonstration reel. It does not redistribute source imagery,
terrain rasters, exact control points or real-event Mesh downloads.

## Project materials

The Rapid3D narrative, page design and project-generated diagrams are copyright © 2026
Zayne1119 / Rapid3D GeoHazard. No project-level open-source licence has been selected yet.

## Upstream data acknowledged by the underlying R&D project

- NASA NSIDC High Mountain Asia DEM inputs.
- Copernicus DEM GLO-30 terrain inputs.
- SRTM data courtesy of the U.S. Geological Survey, accessed through the public
  Mapzen/Tilezen terrain archive.
- Copernicus Emergency Management Service Rapid Mapping event products.
- Planet Labs PBC Crisis Response imagery.
- Vantor Open Data imagery.
- OpenStreetMap contributors where referenced in internal analyses.
- D-Claw, used under its upstream BSD-3-Clause licence.

Planet and Vantor crisis-response imagery and imagery-derived products may be subject to
CC BY-NC 4.0 or provider-specific terms. Copernicus Emergency products can also contain
underlying imagery whose redistribution rights differ from the vector product licence.
Nothing in this repository changes those terms.

## What the public visuals are

The images and video in `docs/assets/` are presentation-scale, non-georeferenced renders of
project-generated terrain, numerical simulation, sensitivity and validation outputs. They
are provided to explain the software and research workflow. They are not source datasets,
survey products, official maps or hazard forecasts.

## Safety boundary

The project does not authorize use of these materials for emergency response, evacuation,
engineering design, insurance decisions or operational risk assessment. Follow competent
authorities and independently validated products for any real-world safety decision.
